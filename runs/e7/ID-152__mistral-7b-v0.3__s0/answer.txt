 1. Strimzi `KafkaTopic` resources for all topics:

```yaml
apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: Kafka
metadata:
  name: my-cluster
spec:
  kafka:
    replicas: 3
    listeners:
    - name: plain
      port: 9092
      tls: false
      config:
        advertisingListenerEnabled: "true"
        listenerType: "PLAINTEXT"
        listenerSecurityPolicy: "PLAINTEXT_LISTENER"
        zookeeperConnect: "zookeeper:2181"
        bootstrapServer: "my-cluster-kafka-bootstrap:9092"

---
apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: KafkaTopic
metadata:
  name: payments
spec:
  replicas: 3
  partitions: 10
  config:
    retention.ms: 604800000
    segment.bytes: 10485760
    compression.type: "lz4"

---
apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: KafkaTopic
metadata:
  name: payments-validated
spec:
  replicas: 3
  partitions: 10
  config:
    retention.ms: 604800000
    segment.bytes: 10485760
    compression.type: "lz4"
```

Explanation:
- We have a Kafka cluster with 3 brokers and a plain listener.
- The `payments` and `payments-validated` topics have 10 partitions each, which allows for a total throughput of 500,000 events/s (5 MB/s per partition).
- The segment.bytes and retention.ms configurations ensure that the topics retain data for 7 days and have a segment size of 10 MB.
- Compression is set to LZ4 to reduce the storage footprint.
