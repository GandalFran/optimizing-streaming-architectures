### 1. Strimzi KafkaTopic resources

```yaml
apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: KafkaTopic
metadata:
  name: ad-impressions
spec:
  kafka:
    bootstrap:
      service:
        name: my-cluster-kafka-bootstrap
        port: 9092
    replicas: 3
  partitions: 16
  replicationFactor: 3
  topic:
    numPartitions: 16
    replicationFactor: 3
    config:
      cleanup.policy: compact
      retention.bytes: 1024*1024*1024*1024
      retention.ms: 86400000
      segment.ms: 300000

---
```
