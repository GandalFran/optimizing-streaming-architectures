 1. Strimzi `KafkaTopic` resources for all topics:

```yaml
apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: Kafka
metadata:
  name: my-cluster
spec:
  kafka:
    replicas: 3
    listeners:
    - name: plain
      port: 9092
      tls: false
      authentication: none
    zookeeper:
      enabled: false
    kafkaRaft:
      enabled: true

---

apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: KafkaTopic
metadata:
  name: ad-impressions
spec:
  replicas: 3
  partitions: 10
  compaction:
    strategy: compact
  config:
    retention.ms: 86400000
    segment.bytes: 10485760
    max.message.bytes: 1048576
    cleanup.policy: delete

---

apiVersion: kafka.strimzi.io/v1beta2
kind: KafkaTopic
metadata:
  name: ad-impressions-enriched
spec:
  replicas: 3
  partitions: 10
  compaction:
    strategy: compact
  config:
    retention.ms: 86400000
    segment.bytes: 10485760
    max.message.bytes: 1048576
    cleanup.policy: delete
```

Explanation:
- `my-cluster` is the Kafka cluster with 3 brokers and KRaft enabled for high availability.
- `ad-impressions` and `ad-impressions-enriched` are Kafka topics with 10 partitions each, configured for retention of 1 day and a maximum message size of 1 MB.
